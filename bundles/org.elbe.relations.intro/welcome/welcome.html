Welcome to Relations

Overview

The Relations application is a tool for knowledge management. It is a combination of a personal Wiki and an electronic slip box.

You can easily create items of three types (terms, texts and persons) and set them in relation to other items already entered, thus creating a rich context for your items.

First Steps

New to Relations? The help explains how to create items, how to relate them and how to navigate the context of an item.
